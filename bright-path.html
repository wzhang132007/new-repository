About Bright Path

Bright Path was inspired by volunteering at a psychiatric hospital and learning about art therapy for autistic children.

This interactive book uses:

🎵 Music to soothe emotions and express feelings

💡 Light to practice focus and cause-and-effect

🧩 Games & Stories to support social skills and confidence

Chapter 1 – Piano of Feelings

Touch the keys to hear gentle notes. Ask your child which sound feels happy, calm, or excited.

Chapter 2 – Bedroom of Light

Turn the slider to change the bedroom light. Talk about bright and dark.

Tips for Parents

Use the piano page to talk about feelings connected to sounds.
Use the light page to practice focus and cause-and-effect.
Use the matching and story pages to practice turn-taking and simple social situations.
